RX 7000 vs RX 9000: what you are choosing between

The RX 7000 series (RDNA 3) is AMD's previous generation and still holds up well at the budget end, especially the RX 7600 for 1080p and the RX 7900 XTX at the very top for its 24GB of memory. The RX 9000 series (RDNA 4) is the current generation, with better ray tracing, improved FSR support, and stronger performance per watt. In the mid-range, the newer RX 9060 XT and RX 9070 cards generally give you more for your money than the older 7000 parts, which is why our mainstream and upper-mid picks below lean on the 9000 series.
