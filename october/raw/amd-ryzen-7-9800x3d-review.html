Yes, if gaming is your main workload and you are pairing it with a high-end graphics card. What second-gen 3D V-Cache actually changes

On the earlier X3D chips, including the 7800X3D, AMD stacked the extra cache die on top of the CPU cores. That cache layer sat between the cores and the cooler, so heat had a harder path out and clock speeds had to be held back. On the 9800X3D, AMD flipped the arrangement and placed the cache underneath the core die (the CCD). The cores now sit directly under the heat spreader.

In practice, that brings three changes you can feel. The chip runs higher clocks than the 7800X3D (5.2GHz boost against 5.0GHz, and a much higher base clock). It is unlocked for overclocking, which the 7800X3D was not. And it keeps the same 96MB L3 pool that makes X3D chips so good in games, especially in CPU-heavy titles like simulators, strategy games and competitive shooters where the GPU is waiting on the processor.

Is the 9800X3D good for streaming and video editing?

It is good, not the best. Eight fast Zen 5 cores handle gaming plus streaming comfortably and are capable for editing. If rendering and heavy multi-threaded work matter as much as gaming, a 12-core chip like the Ryzen 9 9900X will finish those jobs faster, while the 9800X3D stays ahead in games.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Ryzen 7 9800X3D price in India?

The Ryzen 7 9800X3D is listed at ₹43,999 at Avika Retails at the time of writing. Prices on X3D chips change often, so check the product page for today's price and stock.

Is the Ryzen 9800X3D better than the 7800X3D?

Yes. Both have 8 cores and 96MB of L3 cache, but the 9800X3D uses the newer Zen 5 architecture, runs higher clocks (up to 5.2GHz against 5.0GHz) and is unlocked for overclocking. The 7800X3D at ₹31,999 is still the better value if the saving lets you buy a faster graphics card.

Which motherboard should I buy for the Ryzen 7 9800X3D?

Any AM5 motherboard with a BIOS that supports Ryzen 9000 works. B850 boards like the Asus B850M AYW Gaming WiFi support it out of the box, while B650 boards like the ASUS B650M-AYW WiFi or Asus Prime B650M-A CSM may need a BIOS update first.

Does the Ryzen 7 9800X3D come with a cooler?

No. The 9800X3D ships without a cooler and has a 120W TDP, so plan for a capable tower air cooler or a 240mm or larger liquid cooler.

Does the 9800X3D need DDR5 RAM?

Yes. The 9800X3D uses the AM5 socket, which supports DDR5 memory only. A 32GB dual-channel kit at 6000MHz with an AMD EXPO profile is the usual choice for a gaming build.
