RTX 5060 vs RTX 4060: which is better?

The RTX 5060 is the better card. It has 25% more CUDA cores (3,840 vs 3,072), GDDR7 memory with about 65% more bandwidth (448 GB/s vs 272 GB/s), a PCIe 5.0 interface, and DLSS 4 Multi Frame Generation, which the RTX 4060 cannot run. The RTX 4060 still makes sense only if you find it clearly cheaper, since both cards share the same 8GB VRAM limit.

What the differences mean for your build

More cores and much faster memory

The RTX 5060 has 768 more CUDA cores and a newer architecture, so expect it to be faster in native rendering in most games. The bigger change is memory: on the same 128-bit bus, GDDR7 at 28 Gbps gives it 448 GB/s against 272 GB/s on the RTX 4060's GDDR6, which helps most at higher settings.

DLSS 4 Multi Frame Generation vs DLSS 3

On the RTX 5060, DLSS 4 can generate up to three extra frames for every rendered frame in supported games. The RTX 4060 is limited to DLSS 3 Frame Generation, one generated frame per rendered frame. Two honest caveats: generated frames look smoother but do not make the game respond faster to your mouse, and frame generation works best when the base frame rate is already reasonable. The newer DLSS 4 upscaling model also runs on RTX 40 cards; only Multi Frame Generation is RTX 50 only.

8GB VRAM on both

Neither card fixes the biggest weakness of this class: 8GB of video memory. At 1080p with sensible textures it is workable; at 1440p or with ultra textures in newer AAA titles it can run out on either card. For 1440p, a 16GB card is the safer bet, which is why the picks below lean that way.

Power and PCIe lanes

Neither card needs a big power supply (see the FAQ below). Both use only eight PCIe lanes, so on an older PCIe 3.0 board such as A520 or B450 they get half the link bandwidth they were designed around, which hurts most when VRAM fills up. A PCIe 4.0 board like B550 avoids most of that; our best B550 motherboards guide covers good options.

Frequently asked questions

Is the RTX 5060 better than the RTX 4060?

Yes. The RTX 5060 has 3,840 CUDA cores against 3,072, GDDR7 memory with 448 GB/s of bandwidth against 272 GB/s, a PCIe 5.0 interface, and support for DLSS 4 Multi Frame Generation. Both cards have 8GB of VRAM, so neither is ideal for heavy 1440p gaming.

Can the RTX 4060 use DLSS 4?

Partly. RTX 40 series cards, including the RTX 4060, support the newer DLSS 4 upscaling model and DLSS 3 Frame Generation, which adds one generated frame per rendered frame. DLSS 4 Multi Frame Generation, which generates up to three extra frames, is exclusive to RTX 50 series cards like the RTX 5060.

Is 8GB of VRAM enough in 2026?

For 1080p gaming with sensible texture settings, 8GB is still workable. For 1440p or ultra textures in newer AAA games, 8GB can run out, which affects the RTX 5060 and RTX 4060 equally. A 16GB card gives more headroom.

Which power supply do I need for an RTX 5060 or RTX 4060?

The RTX 5060 is rated at 145W total graphics power and the RTX 4060 at 115W, so a good quality 550W to 650W power supply from a known brand is enough for a typical gaming build with either card.
