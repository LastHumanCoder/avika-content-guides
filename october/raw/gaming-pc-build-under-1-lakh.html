Be clear about the trade-off. This build runs one RAM stick in single channel and an entry-level 6GB GPU, so it will game at 1080p but below the AM4 build. Upgrading to the RX 7600 takes it to ₹1,00,183, just over budget. Choose AM5 only if you plan to add a second matching 16GB stick and a stronger GPU within a year. Should you buy AM4 or AM5 for a gaming PC under 1 lakh?

AM4 if you want the best frame rates for the money today: cheaper DDR4 lets you afford an RX 9060 XT. AM5 if you are buying a platform for the next few years and are happy to start with a weaker GPU and single-channel RAM, then upgrade both later.
