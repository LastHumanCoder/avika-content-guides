What decides gaming performance in a laptop

The GPU tier: RTX 4050 vs RTX 4060 vs RTX 4070

The laptop GPU does most of the work in games. The RTX 4050 laptop GPU has 6GB of VRAM and suits 1080p at medium to high settings. The RTX 4060 laptop GPU has 8GB and is the sweet spot for 1080p and 1200p at high refresh rates. The RTX 4070 laptop GPU has more shader cores than the 4060 but the same 8GB of VRAM, so it helps most on QHD screens, though the gap is smaller than the name suggests.

TGP: why the same GPU performs differently

TGP (total graphics power) is the wattage a laptop maker allows the GPU to use. Nvidia lets manufacturers set it across a wide range, from around 35W in thin designs to well over 100W, with Dynamic Boost, in thicker gaming chassis. A high-TGP RTX 4060 can get close to a low-TGP RTX 4070, so always ask for the TGP before you compare prices. We can check it for any model on WhatsApp.

CPU: H vs HX processors

Intel H-series chips, such as the Core i7-13620H and i5-12500H, are designed for mainstream laptops and are plenty for gaming. HX chips are built from desktop-class silicon with more cores and a much higher power budget: the i7-13650HX has 14 cores to the i7-13620H's 10. HX helps with streaming, rendering and CPU-heavy games, but needs stronger cooling. Our laptop processor guide to Intel Core i3, i5 and i7 explains the wider choice.

Display refresh rate

A 144Hz to 165Hz FHD or WUXGA screen matches an RTX 4050 or RTX 4060. QHD panels at 240Hz, like the AORUS 17 BSF's, need an RTX 4070 or better to show their full benefit. A high-refresh screen on a weak GPU is wasted.

RAM and SSD upgradability

Treat 16GB of RAM as the minimum for gaming in 2026. Many gaming laptops use two RAM slots and have room for a second M.2 SSD, but layouts vary by model, so confirm before you buy. Most laptop M.2 slots take standard 2280 NVMe drives such as the Crucial P310 1TB Gen4 NVMe or ADATA Legend 860 1TB; see our full SSD range.

Cooling, especially in a Delhi summer

A gaming laptop only holds its TGP if it can shed heat. Thicker chassis with bigger fans sustain performance longer. Play on a hard, flat surface, keep the vents clear, and expect fan noise under load. A cooling pad helps only a little.
