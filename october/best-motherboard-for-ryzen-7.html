Best Motherboard for Ryzen 7 9700X & 9800X3D in India 2026

Finding the best motherboard for Ryzen 7 in 2026 means picking an AM5 board, because every current Ryzen 7 desktop chip, from the 7700 and 7800X3D to the newer 9700X and 9800X3D, uses AMD's AM5 socket with DDR5 memory. The good news is that the best motherboard for Ryzen 7 is rarely the most expensive one. These are 8-core chips with sensible power draw, and a well-built B650 or B850 board runs them at full speed. What you pay for as you move up to X870 is connectivity, not frame rates.

This guide explains the B650, B850 and X870 chipsets in plain terms, covers DDR5 EXPO memory and BIOS support for Ryzen 9000, and then lists the best motherboard for Ryzen 7 builds from boards actually in stock at Avika Retails, with real prices from our catalog. If you are still choosing the processor, the Ryzen 7 picks are here too, so you can settle the best motherboard for Ryzen 7 9700X or 9800X3D builds and the CPU in one go.

What is the best motherboard for Ryzen 7 9700X and 9800X3D?

For most gaming builds, a B650 board is enough: the Asus Prime B650M-A CSM at ₹10,620 adds a PCIe 5.0 M.2 slot, 2.5Gb Ethernet and BIOS FlashBack, while the ASUS B650M-AYW WiFi at ₹9,999 is the value pick with Wi-Fi. If you want the newer chipset with a PCIe 5.0 SSD slot as standard, the Asus B850M AYW Gaming WiFi at ₹12,708 is the step up. X870 is only worth it if you need USB4 or a PCIe 5.0 graphics slot.

AM5 chipsets explained: B650 vs B850 vs X870

All AM5 boards share the same socket, so any of them will run any Ryzen 7000 or 9000 chip once the BIOS is current. The chipset decides how much PCIe 5.0 and high-speed USB you get, and the board maker decides the power delivery, Wi-Fi and ports. All of the chipsets below support CPU and memory overclocking.

Does PCIe 5.0 matter for a Ryzen 7 build?

For graphics, not much today: current cards run very well on a PCIe 4.0 x16 slot, which every B650 board has. For storage, PCIe 5.0 matters only if you buy a Gen5 SSD, and for gaming load times the difference over a good Gen4 drive is small. A Gen5 M.2 slot is still worth having if you plan to keep the board for years.

USB4 on X870

The real headline of X870 is mandatory USB4, which gives you 40Gbps ports for fast external SSDs and docks. If you do not use that kind of external hardware, a B650 or B850 board gives you the same gaming performance for less money.

Do you need an X870 motherboard for the Ryzen 7 9800X3D?

No. The 9800X3D runs at full speed on a good B650 or B850 board. Its gaming advantage comes from the large 3D V-Cache on the chip itself, not from the chipset. Choose X870 only for USB4, a PCIe 5.0 graphics slot, or premium features like extra M.2 slots and better Wi-Fi.

DDR5 and AMD EXPO: what memory to pair

AM5 is DDR5 only, so your old DDR4 sticks will not carry over. Look for kits with an AMD EXPO profile, which lets the board set the rated speed and timings with one BIOS setting instead of manual tuning. DDR5-6000 is the speed most builders target for Ryzen 7000 and 9000 chips, and two sticks (2x16GB) are easier to run at full speed than four. BIOS support for Ryzen 9000

B650 and X670 boards launched before the Ryzen 9000 series, so an older board needs a BIOS update before it will boot a 9700X or 9800X3D. Boards made more recently often ship with a compatible BIOS, and B850 and X870 boards launched with Ryzen 9000 support. Before you buy, check that the board's BIOS is recent enough for your exact chip, especially the 9800X3D. A board with BIOS FlashBack, like the Asus Prime B650M-A CSM, can be updated from a USB drive without a CPU installed. If you are unsure, message us on WhatsApp before ordering.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best motherboard for Ryzen 7 9800X3D in India?

For most gaming builds, a B850 or quality B650 board is enough. The Asus B850M AYW Gaming WiFi at ₹12,708 is our pick for the 9800X3D, and the Asus Prime B650M-A CSM at ₹10,620 is a strong lower-cost option once its BIOS is updated. X870 adds USB4 but not gaming performance.

Is a B650 motherboard good enough for the Ryzen 7 9700X?

Yes. The 9700X is a 65W chip, so even an entry B650 board like the ASUS B650M-AYW WiFi at ₹9,999 runs it at full speed. Just make sure the board has a BIOS that supports Ryzen 9000 processors.

What is the difference between B650 and B850?

Both are AM5 chipsets that support Ryzen 7000 and 9000 CPUs, DDR5 and overclocking. B850 makes a PCIe 5.0 M.2 SSD slot standard and launched with Ryzen 9000 support, while on B650 PCIe 5.0 storage depends on the board. For gaming performance, they are the same.

Do I need an X870 motherboard for a Ryzen 7?

No. X870 makes USB4 and a PCIe 5.0 graphics slot standard, which helps with fast external drives and docks, but it does not make a Ryzen 7 faster in games. Browse our motherboards category or ask on WhatsApp for current X870 prices.

Can I use DDR4 RAM with a Ryzen 7 9700X or 9800X3D?

No. AM5 boards support DDR5 only. For Ryzen 7000 and 9000 chips, a 32GB (2x16GB) DDR5-6000 kit with an AMD EXPO profile is the common choice, such as the Acer Predator Vesta II RGB 32GB DDR5-6000 EXPO kit at ₹60,999.
