Is it better to buy a pre-built or build a custom gaming PC in India?

Buy a pre-built if you want a tested machine with one bill, one warranty contact and no assembly risk. Choose a custom build if you have a specific GPU, cabinet or RAM kit in mind, or want to reuse parts you already own. At Avika Retails both come from the same store and the same parts catalog, so the real difference is choice.

Checklist before you buy any pre-built

  • Graphics card model: matters more than RGB fans. "4GB graphics" can mean a GT 1030 or a far faster card.
  • RAM sticks, not just capacity: two sticks run dual-channel; a single stick is slower in many games.
  • Power supply: look for a stated wattage and 80 Plus rating. "Cabinet with PSU" without details is worth asking about before you pay.
  • Motherboard chipset: A520M boards are budget AM4 boards, while B650M boards on AM5 support newer processors and DDR5.
  • Storage size: 240GB and 480GB SSDs fill up quickly with modern games. Budget for a second SSD from our SSD range.
