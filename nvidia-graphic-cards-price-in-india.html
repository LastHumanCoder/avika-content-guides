How to choose an Nvidia GPU by resolution

The cleanest way to pick is to start from your monitor. A 1080p 60Hz to 144Hz display is happy with a budget or mainstream card. A 1440p high-refresh panel wants an upper-mid card. Native 4K, or heavy ray tracing with high frame rates, is where the high-end RTX 5080 and RTX 5090 earn their price. VRAM matters too: 8GB is fine at 1080p, 12GB to 16GB gives you headroom at 1440p and 4K with modern textures.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest Nvidia graphic card in India?

The cheapest Nvidia graphic card we stock is the RTX 3050 6GB (MSI Ventus 2X) at ₹27,999. It is an entry-level 1080p card suited to esports titles and lighter AAA games on medium settings, with low power draw that fits budget builds.

Which Nvidia GPU is best for 4K gaming?

For 4K gaming, the RTX 5080 16GB at ₹1,79,999 delivers high frame rates with ray tracing and DLSS 4, while the flagship RTX 5090 32GB at ₹5,89,999 is the fastest option for uncompromised 4K and heavy creation or AI workloads.

What is the difference between RTX 50 and RTX 40 series?

The RTX 50 series (RTX 5060, 5060 Ti, 5070 Ti, 5080, 5090) is the newer generation with GDDR7 memory and DLSS 4 multi-frame generation. The RTX 40 series, like the RTX 4070, is the previous generation and often offers strong value at a lower price for the same tier.

How much VRAM do I need in a graphics card?

For 1080p gaming, 8GB is enough. For 1440p and future-proofing, aim for 12GB to 16GB, which is why the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB and RTX 4070 12GB are popular. For 4K and content creation, 16GB or the RTX 5090's 32GB gives the most headroom.
