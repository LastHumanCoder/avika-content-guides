Intel Core Ultra: the new Arrow Lake platform

Core Ultra (Series 2, Arrow Lake) is Intel's newest desktop platform on the LGA1851 socket, with improved efficiency and a built-in NPU for AI tasks. These chips need a new motherboard, so they suit fresh builds rather than drop-in upgrades. The lineup runs from the Core Ultra 5 245K up to the 24-core Ultra 9 285K flagship.

Is Intel Core Ultra worth it over 14th gen?

Core Ultra is worth it for a brand-new build that values efficiency, lower power draw, and the on-chip NPU for AI workloads. For pure gaming value, a 14th-gen Core i5 or i7 on the cheaper LGA1700 platform is often still the smarter spend, since Core Ultra needs a new LGA1851 motherboard.
